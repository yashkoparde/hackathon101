# The Idea Index

[← Back to README](../../README.md)

40+ hackathon-ready ideas across 8 categories. 

Here is the secret to this list: **Every single idea here is designed to be finishable.** 
There are no "build a decentralized social network on the blockchain" ideas. These are pragmatic, narrow, scoped-down problems that you can actually build a working demo for in 48 hours.

> [!WARNING]
> Before picking one, read [How to Pick an Idea](../01-HOW-TO-PICK-AN-IDEA.md). The best pick is usually the one your team has real firsthand experience with, not the "coolest" one on this list.

## How Each Idea Is Structured

We don't just give you a prompt. Every idea contains the blueprint to win:

1. **The Problem:** Who feels this, and how painful is it today?
2. **Why It's Worth Building:** What makes it more than a toy?
3. **The Minimum Viable Demo:** Exactly what to build first to secure a baseline score.
4. **The "Wow" Factor (Scope +):** What to add if you have 8 hours left and want to win 1st place.
5. **Anti-pattern (Scope -):** The rabbit hole you should avoid so you don't run out of time.
