## 1. About the Platform

**iNSIGHTS AI** is an advanced intelligence platform created to empower builders, researchers, and student innovators. During a fast-paced hackathon sprint, the biggest bottleneck is often not coding—it is understanding dense problem spaces, parsing complex domain regulations, benchmarking competitor solutions, and finding verifiable data.

### How iNSIGHTS AI Powers Hackathon Teams:
- **Instant Domain Mastery:** Compress days of manual Googling and paper reading into 5 minutes of focused intelligence synthesis.
- **Fact-Checked Problem Evidence:** Give your pitch deck authoritative statistics and real-world citations that judges can verify.
- **Rapid Prototyping Inputs:** Generate structured datasets, API schemas, and predictive hypotheses directly tailored to your problem track.

### 2.1 AI Deep Search
**Deep Search** is engineered to look beyond standard commercial search engine results. It queries technical papers, public datasets, industry whitepapers, and global repositories to answer complex multidimensional questions.
- **Hackathon Use Case:** Find verified gaps in existing solutions.
- **Example Query:** *"What are the primary reasons why automated crop disease detection apps fail when deployed in rural Karnataka?"*
- **Outcome:** Highlights lighting variations, regional vernacular dialects, and lack of offline connectivity—giving your team your exact MVP scope.

### 2.2 Document Intelligence
Extract actionable structured information from unstructured, heavy files: academic PDFs, municipal ordinances, institutional datasets, or event problem briefs.
- **Hackathon Use Case:** Rapidly extract requirements, constraints, and tables from 50-page Smart India Hackathon or enterprise PDF briefs.
- **Outcome:** Delivers JSON-compatible tables, summarized regulatory requirements, and technical constraints directly into your team's notes.

### 2.3 PredictX
Explore predictive insights, data-driven trends, and statistical projections to evaluate whether an issue is accelerating over time.
- **Hackathon Use Case:** Validate the business feasibility and market urgency slide of your pitch.
- **Outcome:** Provides empirical projections (e.g., year-over-year growth in e-waste, student dropout rates, or cloud latency metrics) to substantiate your project's long-term viability.

## 3. The 6-Step Hackathon Workflow with iNSIGHTS AI

Integrate iNSIGHTS AI into your team's workflow from the opening bell to the final pitch:

```
[ Step 1: Select Problem ] 
       │
       ▼
[ Step 2: Deep Search Research ] ──► (Benchmark 10+ existing solutions)
       │
       ▼
[ Step 3: Identify Gaps ] ──────────► (Find what existing apps missed)
       │
       ▼
[ Step 4: Validate via PredictX ] ──► (Verify growth trends & feasibility)
       │
       ▼
[ Step 5: Document Intelligence ] ──► (Extract API specs & sample data)
       │
       ▼
[ Step 6: Build MVP & Pitch ] ──────► (Cite verified sources in presentation)
```

### Step 1: Problem Selection
Review available hackathon tracks or open themes. Pick 2–3 candidate problems that align with your team's interest.

### Step 2: Research Problem & Existing Solutions
Use **AI Deep Search** on `insights-ai.info` to explore the selected domains. Uncover what startups, government bodies, and open-source communities have already built.

### Step 3: Identify Gaps & Opportunities
Pinpoint the exact failure points of current tools: Are they too expensive? Do they lack mobile responsiveness? Are they in English only? Your MVP must target this exact gap.

### Step 4: Validate the Proposed Solution
Use **PredictX** to confirm feasibility. Formulate a hypothesis and examine historical and predictive trajectories.

### Step 5: Extract Technical Specifications
Feed domain documents or problem PDFs into **Document Intelligence** to retrieve structured entities, edge cases, and parameters needed for your database design.

### Step 6: Build, Test & Pitch
Build your core prototype. In your pitch presentation, explicitly show how your research was guided by data and verified intelligence from iNSIGHTS AI.

## 4. INVICTUS Event Hands-On Guidelines
