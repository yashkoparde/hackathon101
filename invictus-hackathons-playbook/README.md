## 🚀 5. From Hackathon to Real Product

A hackathon project doesn't have to end at the submission deadline:

1. **User Validation:** Conduct 10 structured user interviews with people experiencing the core problem.
2. **Refactor & Decouple:** Replace hardcoded hacks and prototype mocks with scalable database schemas and error boundaries.
3. **Distribution & Community:** Open-source your codebase, publish an engineering blog post, and share in student developer communities.
4. **Incubation & Grants:** Apply for student startup grants, college entrepreneurship cells (E-Cells), and angel incubators.
